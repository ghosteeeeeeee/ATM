  - → LOSS_COOLDOWN direction (worse after losses) supported in 3 significant cell(s), Wallace in 0.

## Caveats

- Fisher/Welch assume independent trades; trades within a unit are clustered — p-values are optimistic under clustering.
- entry_streak computed per unit on the full window; OOS split does not re-derive streaks inside each partition (state carries across the cut).
- pnl_usdt == 0 (breakeven) counted as non-win for streaks; the live cooldown only increments on pnl < 0 and only clears on pnl > 0.
- Historical max streak is in-sample, never a bound (Wallace known-flaw note).
- This is observational: entries that survived the cooldown are selected — it cannot prove what blocked trades would have done.
