## Approximation caveats (explicit)

1. **MAE/wick touch ≈ stop fill at exactly −S.** No slippage, gap, or funding; a real
   gap-through fills worse than −S.
2. **No intra-bar path ordering.** If both adverse and favorable extremes occur, the
   counterfactual cannot order them; any trade whose path worst breached −S is credited
   the stop. For a winner that dipped below −S *after* its trail had already raised the
   real stop, the static-stop counterfactual kills it where the live system would not
   (overstates kills); conversely live ATR/structured stops sit at different distances
   per trade and can fire later than a uniform −S (understates some losses). Both
   directions uncalibratable at n=47.
3. **Trades whose worst adverse < S keep their original outcome** even though under a
   width-S regime they would not have exited when they did — their subsequent path is
   unknown (bias both ways). This mainly bites at large S: at S=6% no stop-cluster loser
   touched −6% margin, so all keep their original −3~−6% losses (≈ baseline by construction).
4. **Path-worst = max(mae_pct, wick extremes from highest/lowest, realized loss).**
   Recorded snapshots can miss the final exit move: 10 rows where the wick extreme is
   ≥0.01pp worse than mae_pct, 2 winners (16877, 16880) exited beyond their recorded MFE,
   some losers lost more than their recorded worst. The realized loss is floored in
   (variant B, primary); variant A (recorded path-worst only) is reported as sensitivity
   — **same conclusion, same best S** (+$0.05).
5. **pnl_usdt is GROSS of fees** (checkpoint basis kept): parsed fees sum ≈ **$0.47**
   over 45/47 rows (2 null) → net-of-fees baseline ≈ **−$1.10**. Fees are identical
   across every S (same trades, same size, both sides paid), so sweep *deltas* are
   unaffected; only the level shifts.
6. **S is selected in-sample** on the same 47 trades (multiple comparisons). The peak
   is not out-of-sample validated.
7. **Data warts:** (a) hard_tp 16823 / hard_sl 16841 store `pnl_pct` on notional basis
   while 45/47 use margin basis — sum pnl_pct mixes bases slightly; (b) 1 row (16940)
   `pnl_usdt` is 1 cent off the raw×amount identity (likely net-of-fee rounding).
   Canonical `pnl_usdt` sums reproduce the checkpoint exactly, so neither affects findings.

## 6. Verdict (with confidence)

**(a) Is there a stop width where the same 47 trades go net positive, robustly?**
No. A width exists — S=2.0% (variant B: +$0.05; variant A: +$0.05; S=2.25: +$0.04) —
but it is not robust: 1 of 8 prescribed widths (2 of 10 with fine sweep) crosses zero,
and by ≈$0.05 (breakeven, not profit); neighbors (1.75, 2.5) are negative; the recent
half is negative at every width; two of three killed winners sit within 0.2pp of the
threshold. The cell axis does hold (broad improvement, no cell worsens), so it is not
one lucky *cell* — it is one lucky *width*, and it does not survive the time split.

**(b) Strong enough to recommend a constant change to T?**
No — and this is the honest answer the CEO asked for:
- The **diagnosis is strong**: stop cluster median −3.51% vs winner median +1.37%
  (payoff 0.44), 14/18 stops beyond −3% while 21/27 winners finish below +2% —
  the stops ARE too wide relative to what the PM trail lets winners keep, and stop
  width explains essentially the entire −$0.63 bleed (recap at −2% recovers +$0.68 ≈ 107%).
- The **prescription is weak**: the best width buys ≈$0.00 net, *drops* WR from 57.4%
  to 51.1% (which would trip the A-rule's ≥52% WR limb even as it passes the PnL limb),
  fails the time split, and rests on an MAE-touch approximation without intra-bar
  ordering, in-sample S selection, n=47.
- **Recommendation: no stop/constant change this run** (consistent with decision B).
  If T wants the lever, ship it as shadow counterfactual per the
  automatic-implementation pattern: keep a parallel −2% (or regime-scoped — the bleed is
  HIGH/EXTREME SHORT; NORMAL SHORT is already +$0.26) counterfactual ledger alongside
  live exits and adjudicate with real counterfactual fills at the 2026-12-06 deadline.

**Confidence:** high that the stop cluster is the bleed mechanism and that no width
produces real profit on this sample; medium at best on −2.0% being *the* number (fragile
to sub-0.2pp threshold effects and the half2 flip); high that changing constants now
would be overfitting n=47.
