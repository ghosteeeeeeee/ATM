## TLDR

**(a) Detection-side, not execution-side.** Of the 146 `entered_past_mean` trades, 140 have `price_at_signal`; **121/140 (86.4%) were already past the 15m mean at DETECTION** — the signal fired with price already on the wrong side. Only **19/140 (13.6%) decayed between detect and execute**, and that window is tiny: median staleness **1.12 min**, median price move detect→execute **0.105%**. Detection-time z (1m/20) was already wrong-signed in 99/140. Bug location: the detection logic itself, upstream of execution.

**(b) Gate-gap one-liner.** Of the 146, the only price-move gate (`SIGNAL_STALENESS_PRICE_PCT`, decider_run.py:5057) is **dead code** — `price` is seeded from `sig['price']` at :4996 then compared against `sig['price']` at :5054, so drift ≡ 0 whenever the signal carries a price and the check is skipped when it doesn't (0 `[DRIFT]` firings in all logs vs 10,548 `[STALE-WARN]` in the same span); the 5-min age gate is **warn-only** since 2026-09-04 (never blocks); the `is_stale` soft penalty's z comes from `_get_token_zscore()` which **hard-returns 0.0** (signal_gen removed), so "z contradicts regime" is never true. Net: **114/146 stale entries pass every existing gate even counterfactually-live**, and none of the gates measures position-in-band anyway — 121/140 fired already past the mean with median drift 0.105%.

**(c) Outcomes: null.** past n=146 WR **59.6%** mean **−$0.0010** vs clean n=245 WR **56.7%** mean **−$0.0023** — Fisher **p=0.598**, Welch **p=0.907**. TRAIN <2026-09-28 p=0.90/0.60; OOS ≥2026-09-28 past is *better* (56.1% vs 46.2%, Fisher **p=0.406**, Welch **p=0.158**, ns). ## 6. Caveats

1. **Clustered trades.** 64 unique tokens among 146 past trades (NXPC/DOT ×7); naive Fisher/Welch treat trades as independent. Token-cluster bootstrap CI [−0.022, +0.025] includes 0 — clustering does not create or destroy the null.
2. **Metadata coverage is era-dependent and disjoint.** `staleness_minutes` injection started 2026-09-18; `signal_created_at` backfill ran 2026-09-09 and covers only Sept trades; the two never co-occur (both=0). The two age clocks disagree by ~20× (median 1.12 vs 21.3 min) — reported separately, not reconciled; the gate reads the claimed row's `created_at` (= the staleness clock).
3. **Trap-penalty counterfactual is underpowered**: only 12/146 have `coin_regime`+conf (stamped from 2026-10-22, brain_auditor #33); the 1/146 catch is a lower bound on that subgroup, and the gate is dead anyway (z≡0.0).
4. **Log evidence window**: `[DRIFT]`/`[STALE-WARN]`/`counter_trend_trap` counts cover 2026-10-30 20:39 → 2026-11-19 (4 retained log files), not the full 90d trade window. The dead-code proof is structural (code reading + git blame); logs are corroboration only.
5. **Sub-split tests are exploratory**: det-past vs decayed (n=19, Fisher p=0.0049), OOS-excl-last-30d (post-hoc slice, p=0.017) — multiple comparisons, not pre-registered; reported as stability diagnostics, not findings.
6. **OOS power**: test side n=41/52; last-30d n=12/7 (pool dead since 2026-11-01 — SQL `NOW()-30d` total = 19 trades). The last-30d "harm" cannot be confirmed or extended.
7. 2 trades skipped (token `AR`, no 15m warmup), same as result-1; all numbers deterministic given the reproduce blocks.
8. `signal_created_at` >15min for 36/63 of those trades yet they executed despite the 15-min gate being live since 2026-09-03 — either the backfill matched a different row than the gate read, or `sig_id` was None (bypass at :6111). Not resolvable from retained data; the gate's own clock (`staleness_minutes`, max 10.1 min) says 0 would trip.
9. **No proposal is made here.** Per brief: measurement + gate-gap table only.
