## Provenance caveats (stated honestly, repeated in `brain/kill_lines.md`)

1. **REALIZED, not TESTED.** No per-source backtested drawdown exists in this repo;
   the baseline is the source's realized cumulative-PnL drawdown on live trade
   history (PG brain `trades`, `status=closed`). The brief's "worst *tested* DD"
   wording cannot be satisfied today — **every line is marked `provisional: true`**,
   "provisional until a backtest baseline exists".
2. **A historical max is never a hard bound** (Wallace mining rule). The validate
   column already proves it: `r2v2-long3` train DD 0.23 → line 0.46, but its
   validate-window DD is **0.66 — the line was exceeded out-of-sample on day one
   of the split**. Crossing = alert, not proof of a broken source.
3. Thin-sample lines sit on the `n_train ≥ 10` floor (10–13 trades for
   `rr-struct-v2+`, `hzscore`, `r2v2-long3`, `ai-trader-manual`) — weakest lines in
   the registry; a backtest baseline should replace them first.
