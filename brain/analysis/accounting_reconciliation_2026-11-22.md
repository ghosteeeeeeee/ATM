### 4a. `exit_slippage_pct` — actual definition (live PG DDL)

`exit_slippage_pct` is a **GENERATED ALWAYS ... STORED** column. No script writes it — PostgreSQL computes it on every INSERT/UPDATE:

```sql
CASE WHEN ((stop_loss > (0)::numeric) AND (exit_price IS NOT NULL)) THEN ((abs((exit_price - stop_loss)) / stop_loss) * (100)::numeric) ELSE NULL::numeric END
```

It measures **distance-from-stop-at-exit as a % of the stop level**, NOT fill slippage. Winners trail away from the stop (large values), losers exit AT the stop (small values) — that construction alone produces the diagnosis doc's "winners slip 2.7x more than losers" anomaly. It also double-counts for stop-family exits (the exit IS the stop -> 0) and cannot be summed into a friction budget.
