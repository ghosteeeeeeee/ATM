6. **Rewrite C3's conclusion as a hypothesis, not a finding:** composition TV 0.12–0.36, pass-proxy 4.5%→1.0%, known pre-Oct-22 undercount, and no signal-volume denominator — "upstream starvation" is plausible but untested (the report's own §9 says so).

Fix "0.4%"→0.5%, "hourly-sampled"→30-min, and re-check §5's kill-differential pp values against the summary.

Nothing in this list overturns the two central conclusions — PUMP-CHAIN-RSI-MAX is a real protective gate (robust to baseline, bootstrap, OOS, and my independent recomputation) and no gate is demonstrably blocking legitimate setups **at the effect sizes this test can see** — but items 1–3 are paper-trail defects an external auditor will find in the first hour, and items 5–7 change how the headline classes should be read.
