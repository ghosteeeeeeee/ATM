## Overall verdict

**PASS with findings — the shipped change is correct and safe on its own terms; the doctrine claim around it is overstated.**

Proven: (1) the new threshold is exactly the stop's own distance and, by `min()` sign + a 900-case sweep, **HML can never fire before the structure stop** for any valid managed geometry; (2) at 10x the two share one price line and the **stop check runs first** (`:3632` vs `:4224` + `continue`, plus 4dp rounding puts HML ≥0.011pt later) → label `manual_sl_hit` always wins, neither path broken; (3) non-managed, stop-less and wrong-side behavior is **byte-identical** to pre-commit (grid proof); (4) blast radius = only struct > 8/lev (3 of 74 managed trades in 30d, #16909 the only `hard_max_loss`); (5) #16909 would **not** have died at −0.99%, would still be open now at −0.17% price / −1.73% account, with a disclosed worst case of **−24.26% account** if 66670 breaks; (6) regression: suite 20/20, py_compile, fresh imports, harness 33/33, ONE live run_cycle rc=0 (BRACKET/CHOP, tweaks=[], keeper armed long 66933, book flat), 0 Tracebacks from shipped code; (7) scope exactly `scripts/position_manager.py`.

Not proven (F-1/F-2): "worst case = the stop's own distance" — false system-wide: **guardian CUT_LOSER −3.0% price** (non-BTC, struct>3%), **stale_exit −1.0%+8min stall**, weather-vane **30-min** force-close, `wave_turn`, and the latent `cut_loser` priority-3 (−4/lev % price, 0/74 exposure) can each close a managed structural trade **before** its structure stop. None were changed by this commit — but the commit message's enumeration of bounds is incomplete and the docs still advertise the retired floor in 5 places.
