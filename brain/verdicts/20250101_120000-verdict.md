## Claim 3: "Hysteresis at 45 was too tight"

### Verdict: **AGREE — 45 is too close to the noise floor**

### Evidence

From `hermes_constants.py` (lines 955-956):
```python
SNIPER_HYSTERESIS_LONG = 55   # suppress BEARISH exits (close LONGs)
SNIPER_HYSTERESIS_SHORT = 45  # suppress BULLISH exits (close SHORTs)
```

The dead zone is 45-55 (10 points wide). Analysis:

1. **5 trades exited in the 44-47 zone** — all but 1 lost money (20% WR)
2. **The velocity at exit was near-zero** (±0.05) — the trend wasn't decisively moving
3. **L3 exits at score 45-46 are the most dangerous** — they're firing on maximum signals but the hysteresis isn't protecting them

The code logic (lines 551-598):
- BEARISH exits suppressed if score > 55 (protects LONGs)
- BULLISH exits suppressed if score < 45 (protects SHORTs)

**The problem:** When score is 45-46, the SHORT-side hysteresis (score < 45) doesn't fire, so BULLISH exits proceed. But the score is so close to the threshold that it's within noise. A 1-point score change would toggle the protection.

**Evidence from L3 trades:**
- L3-BULLISH exits (SHORT positions closed): 0/5 winners = 0% WR
- These trades had scores of 45-84 at exit
- The hysteresis at 45 didn't protect any of them

**Confidence:** HIGH — The data shows the dead zone is too narrow for L3 signals.
