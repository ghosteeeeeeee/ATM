## Claim 1: "Score 60-80 LONG WR = 65.8%"

**Verdict: DISAGREE**

**My Evidence:**
- PostgreSQL: 60-80 LONG = 19 trades, 14 wins, **73.7% WR**, avg PnL +1.98%
- Plan says 65.8% — actual is 73.7%

**Confidence: LOW** — Only 19 trades with btc_score in metadata out of 2,178 LONG trades total. The sample is extremely small and not statistically significant. Both numbers could be noise.

**Notes:** The `_signal_metadata` column only has btc_score for 146 out of 5,091 total trades (2.9%). This makes ALL score-zone claims statistically unreliable.

## Claim 3: "Velocity predictive = 69-72%"

**Verdict: PARTIAL**

**My Evidence:**
- continuum.db (36,342 BTC entries):
  - Positive velocity → score goes UP at:
    - 3 minutes: **69.5%** ✓ (in range)
    - 5 minutes: **67.1%** ✗ (below range)
    - 10 minutes: **57.1%** ✗
    - 15 minutes: **51.3%** ✗
  - Negative velocity → score goes DOWN at:
    - 3 minutes: **27.4%** (so 72.6% it goes UP — asymmetric!)
    - 5 minutes: **30.3%** (so 69.7% it goes UP)
    - 10 minutes: **40.8%** (so 59.2% it goes UP)

**Confidence: MEDIUM**

**Notes:** At the 3-minute horizon, positive velocity predicts upward score movement 69.5% of the time — within the claimed 69-72% range. However, the claim is misleading because negative velocity does NOT predict downward movement at the same rate. The asymmetry means "velocity predicts score direction" is only half-true — positive velocity is predictive, negative velocity is not (it's actually more likely to reverse).

## Claim 4: "Score falling SHORT = 79.5% WR"

**Verdict: DISAGREE**

**My Evidence:**
- PostgreSQL: SHORT + momentum_state='falling' = 237 trades, 135 wins, **57.0% WR**
- PostgreSQL: SHORT + momentum_state='flat' = 223 trades, 133 wins, **59.6% WR**
- PostgreSQL: SHORT + momentum_state='rising' = 98 trades, 54 wins, **55.1% WR**
- Plan says 79.5% — actual is 57.0%

**Confidence: HIGH** — 237 trades is a reasonable sample. The claim is off by 22.5 percentage points.

**Notes:** The three momentum states for SHORTs are all in the 55-60% range. There is no dramatic advantage to "score falling" for SHORTs. The 79.5% claim appears fabricated or derived from a different metric not present in the data.

### Critical Data Quality Issue
Only 146 out of 5,091 trades (2.9%) have btc_score in `_signal_metadata`. Any analysis relying on btc_score zones is based on an extremely small, potentially biased sample. The score-zone win rate claims (Claims 1-2) should be treated with extreme caution.

### Fabricated/Inflated Numbers
Claims 4 and 5 (79.5% and 43% for score falling) appear to be fabricated or derived from a metric not present in the current data. The actual momentum_state data shows all states produce 51-60% WR regardless of direction — there is no dramatic 79.5% or 43% outlier.
