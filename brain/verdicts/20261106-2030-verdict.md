## Bottom line

1. **Round-1 verdict of the spec is correct and reproduces:** ATR↑60 marks elevated
   volatility (trivially — state, not event; I confirm events ≤ state at 1h/4h/24h under
   four state constructions), and 1m MACD/RSI direction at the cross is a coin flip that
   loses in the first 15–30 min (p≈0.001–0.004, not the claimed <0.001 at both horizons).
2. **Round-2 R2a is the one live candidate:** magnitude and no-lookahead claims verified
   three ways; but the coin-sign test (the spec's own honesty metric) flips to 9/3/p=0.073
   within a day, effective n is 30 days and ≈3 coins, and the cell was selected from ≥12 on
   the same window. **Shadow-only with ≥30 forward days of day-blocked CI is the only
   defensible next step** — exactly the spec's own recommendation, now with more reason.
3. **R2b/R2c/R2d and the replay deliverable all reproduce exactly**; the replay is safe
   (dynamically proven) but carries a **+6.5 pt bullish bias** vs production that should be
   logged before any replay-score-based signal goes live.
