## Method & verification appendix
- Script: analysis/mfe_mae_8h.py (deterministic, read-only). Window = [open, open+8h];
  extremes over the UNION of candles_1m + candles_5m (candles.db); fav_at_exit from
  trade prices; fav_after = best entry-based favorable level among candles STARTING at
  or after exit (straddling candle excluded → conservative).
- bigger_move (v2) = fav_after >= max(fav_at_exit, 0) + T. v1 flaw found in self-audit:
  losers (negative capture) counted on any mildly favorable tick — v1 numbers discarded.
- Reproducibility: 3 full runs identical (only ALT's live candle count drifts 235→238
  between runs — DB is being appended to by the live collector).
- Independent raw-SQL spot checks (separate code path, from-entry and from-exit bases):
  BTC ugb_pt 10-26 fav_after 0.95 vs 0.97 MATCH; ZRO mae_pk 4.76 MATCH; DYDX 1.97 MATCH;
  WLD 10.07 and GRASS 12.50 reconcile arithmetically (entry-base = cap + exit-base;
  both have 480/480 candles — moves are real, not data gaps).
- Split-half: older 34/47 vs newer 36/47 — phenomenon present in both halves. No formal
  p-value claimed: "exited before the 8h max" is near-tautological (a max over a longer
  horizon usually beats an interim exit) — the meaningful stats are the MAGNITUDES and
  the loser-continuation tail, both reported above.
- Caveats: closed trades only (survivorship on open positions); 4/100 skipped for
  insufficient candles (DOT/BLUR/INJ/ALT — 20-50% coverage); RENDER window 51% coverage
  (extremes may understate); fav/mfe figures are GROSS price moves (fees not deducted);
  scenario 4b exits at the peak for non-stopped trades → UPPER BOUND, not achievable;
  cohort is conditioned on MFE>=0.8% (T's premise), which is outcome-selection for any
  policy claim.
